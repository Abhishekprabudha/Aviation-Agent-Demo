In this three minute executive demo, the original video audio is removed. AIonOS overlays ten aviation AI agents from commercial and business aviation into one transparent command layer. The background accelerates to end exactly with the narration.

Agent one is predictive aircraft maintenance. It listens to vibration, temperature, pressure and oil signals, detects degradation patterns, scores component risk, and auto generates work orders before an aircraft on ground event hits the network.

Agent two is revenue leakage control and yield AI. It scans PSS and DCS data for unbilled ancillaries, fare class mismatches, interline settlement gaps and no show revenue loss, then pushes prioritized recovery queues to revenue teams.

Agent three is passenger CX and contact centre intelligence. Voice, chat, social sentiment, reservation status, loyalty context and OCC events converge so passengers are proactively informed, rebooked and served before call volume spikes.

Agent four is fuel optimisation and carbon intelligence. It compares uplift, climb, cruise, descent, route, aircraft weight and weather, then recommends fuel optimal options while calculating carbon output per tail and route for compliance reporting.

Agent five is crew scheduling and operations AI. It optimises pairing and rostering against duty, rest and demand constraints. During disruption, it ranks recovery options by cost, legality, availability and network impact.

Agent six is on demand scheduling and trip intelligence. Aircraft availability, crew qualification, airport slots, route constraints and NOTAMs are matched instantly to build optimised quotes and booking workflows for private aviation operators.

Agent seven is condition based maintenance. It monitors avionics and airframe sensor data, detects deviation thresholds and recommends maintenance only when health evidence justifies action, improving aircraft availability and reducing unscheduled work.

Agent eight is VIP passenger personalisation. Catering, cabin configuration, ground transport, preferred FBOs and dietary requirements are converted into dynamic preference profiles that update after every journey and trigger service actions automatically.

Agent nine is ground operations turnaround intelligence. It tracks fuelling, catering, cleaning and handling agents against plan, identifies slippage and resequences ramp tasks to protect departure time and reduce incident risk.

Agent ten is AI flight planning and route optimisation. Live weather, NOTAMs, wind patterns and airspace restrictions are fused into sector level recommendations, enabling reroute decisions, lower fuel burn, better flight time and measurable carbon reduction.
